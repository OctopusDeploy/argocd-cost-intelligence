# Governance

## Sponsor

ArgoCD Cost Intelligence is sponsored by [Octopus Deploy](https://octopus.com). Octopus Deploy owns the GitHub repository, the project's name and the infrastructure that builds and publishes releases, and it pays for the time of the maintainers it employs. The project is not part of the Argo project, the CNCF or OpenCost.

## Roles

Contributors are everyone who opens an issue, starts a discussion, sends a pull request, reviews one or answers someone else's question.

Maintainers are listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). They review and merge pull requests, triage issues, look after the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md), cut releases and handle security reports.

## Making decisions

Most decisions are made in the issue or pull request they come from. A maintainer merges a pull request once its checks pass.

A larger decision, such as a change of direction, a breaking change or a change to this document, is proposed in an issue. It is accepted by lazy consensus: if no maintainer objects within a week, it goes ahead. If the maintainers can't agree, a majority of them decides.

## Becoming a maintainer

Anyone can become a maintainer, whether or not they work for Octopus Deploy. What counts is a few months of sustained contributions, whether code, reviews, docs or helping others in issues and discussions.

A maintainer nominates the contributor in an issue, and the contributor becomes a maintainer once the existing maintainers agree. They are then added to [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) and given the access the role needs.

## Stepping down

A maintainer can step down at any time by opening a pull request that removes them from [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md).
